BUXORO SMART QALQON — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-07 · App package: uz.buxoro.kiber_qalqon · v8.0.0 (99) · Contact: murodovichmehriddin@gmail.com

BUXORO SMART QALQON ("the App") is an on-device cybersecurity tool for users in Uzbekistan. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and what (if anything) leaves your device.

Summary: All security analysis happens on your device. We never collect the content of your SMS, notifications, chats, photos, files, contacts, browsing history, or network traffic.

1. Core principle — on-device processing

Security analysis (fraud detection in messaging notifications, app and permission audit, file checks, URL analysis, and prohibited-content filtering) is performed locally on your device. The content of your SMS, messages, notifications, photos, files, and contacts is never uploaded to our servers.

2. Prohibited-content filtering

The App can block access to:

  • Resources declared prohibited by the Supreme Court of the Republic of Uzbekistan (extremist and terrorist material). The list is bundled with the App.
  • Pornographic websites and channels, detected by an on-device keyword and domain list.
  • Online gambling and betting resources, which are prohibited under Uzbek law.

Detection works if you enable Android's Accessibility service, by reading only the text currently visible on screen in browsers and social apps to detect a prohibited domain or channel name. That screen text is analyzed in memory and is never stored or transmitted.

4. Parental control (optional)

If you link a child device to a parent device using a pairing code:

  • When prohibited content is blocked on the child device, the parent receives an alert containing only: the blocked source (site/channel name), the reason, the time, and the child device's name/model.
  • Personal messages, passwords, photos, contacts, keystrokes, precise location and browsing history are never transmitted to the parent or to us.
  • Either side can unlink at any time.

6. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication) — stores the statistics described in §3.
  • VirusTotal (optional) — when checking a file, only its SHA-256 hash (never the file itself) may be sent to check against antivirus engines. The request goes through our own Cloud Function, which forwards only the hash and returns a summary; the API key never reaches your device.

7. Data retention & deletion

Statistics are retained to show aggregate protection metrics. To request deletion of data associated with your device, contact us at the email above. Clearing the optional "Device owner" fields removes that information from future reports.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. The parental-control feature is intended to be configured by a parent or legal guardian.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date reflects the latest version.

BUXORO SMART QALQON — Maxfiylik siyosati (O‘zbekcha)

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-07-31 · Aloqa: murodovichmehriddin@gmail.com

BUXORO SMART QALQON — qurilma ichida ishlaydigan kiberxavfsizlik vositasi. Biz maxfiyligingizni hurmat qilamiz.

Qisqacha: Barcha tahlil qurilmangizda bajariladi. SMS, xabar, surat, fayl, kontakt, brauzer tarixi va internet trafigi mazmuni hech qachon yig‘ilmaydi.

1. Asosiy tamoyil — qurilmada tahlil

Xavfsizlik tahlili (xabar bildirishnomalari, ilovalar, fayllar, havolalar, taqiqlangan kontent) qurilmangizda bajariladi. Shaxsiy mazmun serverga yuborilmaydi.

2. Taqiqlangan kontentni filtrlash

Ilova quyidagilarga kirishni bloklashi mumkin:

  • O‘zbekiston Respublikasi Oliy sudi tomonidan taqiqlangan deb topilgan ekstremistik va terroristik manbalar (ro‘yxat ilova ichida saqlanadi).
  • Pornografik saytlar va kanallar (qurilma ichidagi ro‘yxat va kalit so‘zlar asosida).
  • Onlayn qimor va tavakkalchilikka asoslangan o‘yin saytlari hamda ilovalari.

Aniqlash siz Maxsus imkoniyatlar xizmatini yoqsangiz — brauzer va ijtimoiy ilovalarda ekranda ko‘rinib turgan matn asosida amalga oshiriladi. Bloklash qarori faqat siz OCHGAN manba (ilova panelidagi nom yoki manzil satridagi domen) bo‘yicha qabul qilinadi; qidiruv so‘rovi va shaxsiy yozishma tekshirilmaydi. Bu matn faqat xotirada tahlil qilinadi va saqlanmaydi hamda yuborilmaydi.

4. Ota-ona nazorati (ixtiyoriy)

Farzand qurilmasi juftlash kodi orqali ota-ona qurilmasiga bog‘langan bo‘lsa:

  • Taqiqlangan kontent bloklanganda ota-onaga faqat quyidagilar yuboriladi: bloklangan manba (sayt/kanal nomi), sabab, vaqt va farzand qurilmasining nomi/rusumi.
  • Shaxsiy yozishmalar, parollar, suratlar, kontaktlar, klaviatura kiritmalari, aniq joylashuv va brauzer tarixi hech qachon yuborilmaydi.
  • Bog‘lanishni istalgan tomon istalgan vaqtda uzishi mumkin.

5. Ruxsatlar

QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES — zararli ilovalarni aniqlash; Maxsus imkoniyatlar (ixtiyoriy) — xavfli o‘rnatishni to‘xtatish, soxta Telegram sahifasi, taqiqlangan kontent va onlayn qimor manbalarini aniqlash; POST_NOTIFICATIONS / VIBRATE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE — real-vaqt ogohlantirish va fon monitoringi; REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES — zararli ilovani o‘chirish; INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — qora ro‘yxatni yangilash va anonim statistika; RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED / REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS — qayta ishga tushgach himoyani tiklash.

6. Uchinchi tomon xizmatlari

Google Firebase (statistika), VirusTotal (faqat fayl SHA-256 hash’i — faylning o‘zi emas).

7. Ma’lumotni o‘chirish

Qurilmangiz bilan bog‘liq ma’lumotni o‘chirish uchun yuqoridagi elektron pochtaga murojaat qiling. "Qurilma egasi" maydonlarini tozalash bu ma’lumotni keyingi hisobotlardan chiqaradi.

8. Bolalar

Ilova 13 yoshgacha bo‘lgan bolalarga mo‘ljallanmagan. Ota-ona nazorati funksiyasi ota-ona yoki qonuniy vakil tomonidan sozlanishi kerak.
